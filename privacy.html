Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

DEAD PATTERN Studio ("the app") is a private tool used only by the creator of the DEAD PATTERN channels to publish their own videos. This policy explains what the app does with data from Google (YouTube), Meta (Facebook) and TikTok.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the creator's own channel, and to read the status of those uploads (scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly).
  • Facebook: permission to publish Reels to the creator's own Facebook Page.
  • TikTok: permission to publish videos to the creator's own TikTok account.

How data is used and stored

Access tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer, in a private file readable only by its owner. They are used solely to upload the creator's videos and confirm that the upload succeeded. The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer data about any other person, and it does not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions, Facebook's Business Integrations settings, or TikTok's Manage app permissions. Deleting the local token file removes all stored access.
